    PubMed (NCBI) ↗ClinicalTrials.gov ↗

    Latest research is drawn daily from PubMed: peer-reviewed, English-language papers on atypical teratoid/rhabdoid tumor (AT/RT) and CNS rhabdoid biology, excluding case reports, letters, comments, editorials, preprints and rhabdoid tumors outside the brain. Landmark papers are a curated, verified list. Trials are a ClinicalTrials.gov snapshot from 3 Oct 2026 (condition “rhabdoid”). “Rhabdoid-specific” marks trials whose title names AT/RT, rhabdoid tumor or SMARCB1/INI1 loss; broader trials are pediatric brain-tumor or basket studies that also enroll AT/RT patients and are hidden unless you ask for them. Working-group assignment is automatic, based on each trial’s listed interventions.
